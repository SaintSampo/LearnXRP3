XM Concept C

Competition day in four steps

Organized by time, not by feature: Teams, Schedule, Play, Results. Each step says what to do and when it is done. Play is the FMS with a pre-match checklist and keyboard shortcuts. Results adds awards and certificates. Made for a first-year teacher who has never run a competition.
